In transit

The cold chain is the product.

Ice that arrives fused into a block has been warm. Blue Leaf bags sealed at the facility and holds temperature to the door, so what you open is what was made.

  • Sealed at the facility, not at the kerb
  • Held cold from the cold room to the door
  • Delivery scheduled around your event, not the round

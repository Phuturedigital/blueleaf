About

Clean ice, out of Edenvale.

Blue Leaf Ice Company supplies food-grade ice to homes, events and businesses across Gauteng. Retail bags and bulk, with dependable delivery and pricing published in full.

How it is made

Filtered water. Hygienic facility. Sealed bag.

Ice goes straight into a drink, so it is food, and it is handled that way. Blue Leaf’s ice is food-grade certified, made with filtered water in a hygienic facility, and sealed at the point it is bagged.

  • Food-grade certified
  • Made with filtered water
  • Produced in a hygienic facility
  • Sealed, hygienic packaging

Questions

Your ice delivery questions, answered.

How do I order ice?

Order online or call. Choose your bag size, delivery area and date, and Blue Leaf confirms and arranges the delivery.

What payment methods do you accept?

EFT, credit card, or cash on delivery. Secure online payment links are available.

How soon will my ice arrive?

Same-day delivery in Edenvale and nearby. Other areas are delivered within 24 hours.

Can I update or cancel my order?

Yes. Contact Blue Leaf before dispatch to change or cancel an order.

Is your ice safe for consumption?

Yes. The ice is food-grade certified and made with filtered water in a hygienic facility.

What bag sizes can I buy?

2 kg or 3 kg. Order any quantity, from a single bag to bulk. See the tiers.

Do you charge for delivery?

Free in Zone 0 on orders of five bags or more, R50 flat in Zone 1 and R100 flat in Zone 2. Beyond 20 km from Edenvale it is quoted per trip from R150. Check your suburb.

Is there a discount for event planners?

Yes. Blue Leaf offers event planners a discounted rate on top of the published bulk tiers. More for trade buyers.

About this site

Why this page exists.

This is not Blue Leaf Ice Company’s website. It is an unsolicited design concept built by Phuture Digital, using only information the company already publishes.

The exercise was narrow: take a real small business with a real product and see what changes when the information it already has is put on the page properly rather than rendered into pictures.

  • Their phone number is a tap-to-call link, not pixels in an image
  • Their delivery zones and fees are a readable, searchable table
  • Their four price tiers are text a screen reader can read out
  • Seven pages instead of one, so each thing can be found
  • Photography of real ice production, not generated pictures of it

Every price, suburb and claim was taken from blueleafice.co.za in August 2026. Nothing about the business has been invented, and nothing here is an offer.

Why it matters

Ice is food. It should be made like food.

Blue Leaf makes its ice with filtered water in a hygienic facility, and bags it sealed. That is the difference between ice you can put in a drink and ice you can only put around a bottle.

  • Crystal-clear, food-grade ice
  • Sealed, hygienic packaging
  • Cold chain held from facility to door
  • Dedicated support team

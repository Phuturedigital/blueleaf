Setting it up

Four steps to a standing order.

1. Tell us the run

Bag size, weekly volume, delivery suburb and the days that suit you.

2. Get a written quote

Tier rate plus the delivery fee for your zone. No surprises at the door.

3. Lock the schedule

A repeating slot, or ad-hoc drops when you call them in.

4. Settle how you like

EFT, card on a secure link, or cash on delivery.

Talk volume.

Send your weekly numbers and Blue Leaf will come back with a tier and a schedule.
